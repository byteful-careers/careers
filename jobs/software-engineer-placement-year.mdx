Byteful builds the proxy infrastructure that web scraping, automation and agentic AI companies run on. We process 50+ billion requests a month for thousands of customers, and we got here bootstrapped, to seven figures in ARR.

## About the role

This is a 12-month placement for students taking a year in industry as part of their degree. You will join the engineering team as a full member, not as a spectator. You will ship real code to production, own real work, and learn how a high-traffic platform is built and run. We want you to leave the year as a much stronger engineer than when you arrived, with work you can point to.

Within this role you will:

- Build and ship features across our product, from the API and dashboard to the services behind the proxy network
- Work on the infrastructure that keeps the platform running, including Kubernetes, CI pipelines and observability
- Write tests, review code and take part in how we design, ship and roll back changes
- Investigate production issues with the team, and learn how to debug a distributed system under real load
- Build internal tools that remove manual work for the rest of the team
- Own at least one project from start to finish, from the first design to running it in production

You will have a named mentor on the team and regular one-to-ones. By the end of the year, you will have shipped work that thousands of customers depend on.

## About you

- You are studying for a degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related subject, and your course includes a placement year
- You have written real code outside of coursework, such as personal projects, open source, hackathons or a part-time job
- You are comfortable with at least one programming language, and you are keen to learn more
- You have a basic understanding of Linux, networking and how the web works
- You are curious about how things work under the hood, and you like to find out why something broke
- You can work through a problem on your own, and you know when to ask for help

Bonus points if you:

- Have used Go or Python
- Have used Docker, Kubernetes or any cloud provider
- Run a homelab, a VPS or anything else you had to keep online yourself
- Use AI tools as part of how you work
- Know any of our stack: Ansible, ArgoCD, Kafka, Dragonfly, Postgres, Grafana, Tailscale, Scaleway, DigitalOcean

## Things to know

- We are a small in person team in Manchester. You will be in the office with us
- This is a paid, full-time placement for 12 months
- We are bootstrapped and profitable. There is no runway clock, but there is also no one to hand problems to
- You will own real outcomes. Your code goes to production, and you will see the results
- We move quickly. We prefer judgement over process and leverage over coordination

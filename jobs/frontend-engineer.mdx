We're hiring a Frontend Engineer at Byteful, a fast-growing, bootstrapped proxy infrastructure company based in Manchester.

Our residential proxy network powers web scraping, automation and agentic AI companies worldwide. We support thousands of customers across 195+ countries and process 25+ billion proxy requests every month, with 252% year-on-year growth in platform sign-ups. We've grown to seven figures in ARR without a sales team. Now we want to go much faster.

You will own the products our customers touch every day. That means the website and dashboard, the mobile and desktop apps, and the growing set of proxy tools around them, from browser extensions to fingerprint testing and automation utilities. You'll work closely with our designers to turn wireframes and mockups into polished, fast interfaces, and you'll have a real say in the tools, frameworks and architecture we build them with.

This is a small in person team that moves quickly. Nobody will chase you for progress, and nobody will hand you a finished spec. If you love turning ideas into working products, dig deep into new topics and take full responsibility for what you ship, we'd like to hear from you.

## Responsibilities

1. Turn wireframes and mockups from our designers into functional, polished user experiences
2. Improve the performance and UX of our website and dashboard, and ship new features that make the customer experience better
3. Build and ship cross-platform mobile and desktop apps, from iOS to Windows
4. Expand our proxy tool ecosystem, including browser extensions, fingerprint testing and automation tools
5. Own the products you build from planning to production, and deliver features customers rely on
6. Lead on interface design alongside the team, turning complex workflows into clear, intuitive screens
7. Drive technical decisions on the tools, frameworks and architecture that keep our products fast and easy to extend

## Requirements, must have

- Experience with Next.js, React and UIkit, and comfort navigating and contributing to large codebases
- An eye for design and detail, from layout to micro-interactions
- Hands-on experience building against APIs and connecting frontend to backend
- Figma fluency. You can take a design file and build it pixel for pixel
- Something you've built before. A side project, an app, a browser extension or a tool you couldn't find so you made it yourself

## Requirements, nice to have

- Experience shipping cross-platform mobile or desktop apps
- Experience building browser extensions
- Experience working with designers in a small, fast-moving team
- Startup experience. You know how fast things move when the team is small

## Working arrangement

- Full time, in person at our Manchester office
- Reporting directly to our engineering lead
- Room to grow. You'll own the products you build from planning to production, and shape the tools and frameworks we use as the team grows

Byteful builds the proxy infrastructure that web scraping, automation and agentic AI companies run on. We process 50+ billion requests a month for thousands of customers, and we got here bootstrapped, to seven figures in ARR.

## About the role

This is a summer internship for students who want to find out what engineering at a fast-moving company is really like. You will not spend the summer on a side project that nobody uses. You will join the engineering team, take on one focused project, and ship it to production before you leave.

Within this role you will:

- Own one scoped project for the summer, agreed with your mentor in your first week
- Build and ship code to production, with tests, code review and monitoring
- Work with the team on the API, dashboard, proxy network or infrastructure, depending on your project
- Learn how we deploy, observe and debug a platform that handles billions of requests a month
- Present what you built to the team at the end of the internship

You will have a named mentor on the team and regular check-ins. By the end of the summer, you will have shipped something real and you will know how it runs in production.

## About you

- You are studying for a degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related subject
- You have written real code outside of coursework, such as personal projects, open source contributions, hackathons or a part-time job
- You are comfortable with at least one programming language, and you are keen to learn more
- You have a basic understanding of Linux, networking and how the web works
- You are curious about how things work under the hood, and you like to find out why something broke
- You can work through a problem on your own, and you know when to ask for help

Bonus points if you:

- Have used Go or Python
- Have used Docker, Kubernetes or any cloud provider
- Run a homelab, a VPS or anything else you had to keep online yourself
- Use AI tools as part of how you work
- Know any of our stack: Ansible, ArgoCD, Kafka, Dragonfly, Postgres, Grafana, Tailscale, Scaleway, DigitalOcean

## Things to know

- We are a small in person team in Manchester. You will be in the office with us
- This is a paid, full-time internship for 12 weeks over the summer
- We are bootstrapped and profitable. There is no runway clock, but there is also no one to hand problems to
- You will own a real outcome. Your project goes to production, and you will see the results
